# Public Data Sets Not On Snowflake (Part 2: Census & BEA)

These are some data sets which we have collected but not yet uploaded to Snowflake.

## 5. Census Bureau API Datasets

A. **Employment by State**
* **Prefix:** `census_bureau_employment_by_state_`
* **Years Available:** 2011 through 2022 (excluding 2010)
* **Description:** Annual state-level employment statistics compiled by the U.S. Census Bureau.

B. **Education by State**
* **Prefix:** `census_bureau_education_by_state_`
* **Years Available:** 2012 through 2022
* **Description:** State-level education attainment data from the American Community Survey (ACS).

C. **Population by State**
* **Prefix:** `census_bureau_population_by_state_`
* **Years Available:** 2009 through 2022
* **Description:** Annual population estimates for U.S. states.

D. **Business Economy Summary**
* **Prefix:** `census_bureau_business_economy_summary_`
* **Years Available:** 2017 through 2021
* **Description:** Annual summary statistics on business activity by state.

## 6. Census Bureau Business Trends and Outlook Survey (BTOS)

A. **Census_BTOS National.xlsx**: National-level results capturing firm sentiment.
B. **State.xlsx**: State-specific BTOS metrics.
C. **Top 25 MSA.xlsx**: BTOS data segmented by the top 25 U.S. MSAs.
D. **Employment Size Class.xlsx**: BTOS results disaggregated by business size.
E. **Sector.xlsx**: Survey results categorized by major industry sectors.
F. **Subsector.xlsx**: More granular BTOS statistics based on NAICS subsectors.
G. **Sector by Employment Size Class.xlsx**: Cross-tabulated results.
H. **URR.xlsx**: Unit response rate statistics.

## 7. BEA Consumer Spending

A. **State Personal Income** (`pi0325.xlsx`): Wages, dividends, interest, etc.
B. **Regional Price Parities (RPP)** (`rpp1224.xlsx`): Cost-of-living index data.
C. **State Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE)** (`pce1024.xlsx`): Consumer spending by category.

## 8. BEA Expenditures by State

A. **SAPCE1**: PCE by Major Type of Product (1997–2023).
B. **SAPCE2**: Per Capita PCE by Major Type of Product (1997–2023).
C. **SAPCE3**: PCE by Type of Product (Detailed categories, 1997–2023).
D. **SAPCE4**: PCE by Function (1997–2023).

## 9. BEA GDP Estimates by Quarter

A. **Quarterly GDP**: 2018 Q2 through 2025 Q1. Includes components like consumption, investment, etc.

## 10. BEA Regional GDP

A. **TASUMMARY.zip**: Annual summary of GDP by state.
B. **PRGDP.zip**: Per capita real GDP by state.
C. **SASUMMARY.zip**: Annual GDP data by state with sector-level breakdowns.
D. **SQGDP.zip**: Quarterly GDP estimates by state.
E. **SAGDP.zip**: Annual GDP by state and industry (NAICS).
F. **SAGDP_SIC.zip**: State-level GDP by industry (SIC).
G. **CAGDP1.zip**: Total county-level GDP.
H. **CAGDP8.zip**: County-level GDP for private industries.
I. **CAGDP9.zip**: County-level GDP for government sectors.
J. **CAGDP2.zip**: County-level GDP in chained 2017 dollars.
K. **CAGDP11.zip**: County-level GDP by detailed industry sectors.

## 11. BEA Regional Income

A. **SASUMMARY.zip**: Annual summary of personal income by state.
B. **RPP.zip**: Regional Price Parities for income adjustment.
C. **CAINC1.zip**: County-level personal and per capita income.
D. **CAINC91.zip**: Population and income summary by county.
E. **CAINC4.zip**: Personal current transfer receipts (Social Security, etc.).
F. **CAINC5S.zip**: Earnings by industry (SIC) at county level.
G. **CAINC5N.zip**: Earnings by industry (NAICS) at county level.
H. **CAINC6S.zip**: Compensation by industry (SIC) at county level.
I. **CAINC6N.zip**: Compensation by industry (NAICS) at county level.
J. **CAINC30.zip**: Economic profile of county-level income components.
K. **SQINC.zip**: State quarterly personal income.
L. **SAINC.zip**: State annual personal income statistics.

## 12. Census Bureau API Datasets (County-Level)

A. **Race/Ethnicity by Educational Attainment** (`acs_s1501_raceeth_educ_`): 2015, 2017, 2019, 2021.
B. **Race/Ethnicity Population Summary** (`census_raceeth_`): 2020 Census Redistricting Data.
