# Lightcast Labor Insights Overview

If you want to quickly explore Lightcast Postings data, one great way to do that is by using [Lightcast's Labor Insights](https://login.economicmodeling.com/login/login.php). Labor Insights combines Lightcast data with public data sets to produce a variety of metrics. This allows you to get summarized data quickly, especially for key words not found in our standard data set. To gain access, you will need to get an account set up by our Lightcast contact (as of this writing, Josh LaFonn: joshua.lafon@economicmodeling.com).

## Job Posting Analytics

Labor Insights most useful metrics relate to their job posting data. To access, go to **Job Postings > Job Postings Analytics**. The standard options allow you to look at occupations, regions, and time frames. Advanced options include skills, companies, and the ability to search posts for customized key words.

Once you run the query, you will get a list of measures matching your search. This includes:
* Number of posts over time
* Salary distributions
* Education requirements
* Companies hiring
* Related skills requested in postings and listed by workers in profiles
* Sample of posts meeting criteria

Labor Insights is particularly helpful when looking for specific keywords. Using Snowflake to search for keywords in the body of posts can be very slow, but Labor Insights does this very quickly. It can help clarify what your search terms should be.
